

# Monica – One Page Feature Summary

## What Is Monica?

Monica is an open-source **Personal Relationship Management (PRM)** web application. Think of it as a CRM—but instead of managing customers for sales teams, it helps you track and remember everything about your friends, family, and personal relationships.

## Core Purpose

Document interactions and life details with the people you care about so you don't have to rely solely on memory.

---

## Key Features

| Category | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Contact Management** | Store detailed profiles for each person you know |
| **Interaction Logging** | Record conversations, meetings, and encounters |
| **Life Events Tracking** | Note birthdays, milestones, and important dates |
| **Notes & Reminders** | Keep contextual information about each relationship |
| **Privacy-Focused** | Self-hosted option keeps your data under your control |
| **Open Source** | Free software with transparent code (LGPL license) |

---

## Who Is It For?

- People who want to document their personal lives systematically
- Those who have trouble remembering details about friends/family
- Individuals with Asperger syndrome who benefit from structured social tracking
- People with early-stage Alzheimer's needing memory support
- Introverts seeking tools to maintain relationships

---

## What Monica Isn't

- A social network—it doesn't connect you to strangers or public feeds
- A replacement for genuine interaction—it's meant to support relationships, not automate them
- Commercial surveillance software—your data stays yours (especially when self-hosted)

---

## Technical Details

- **Stack:** Built with Laravel (PHP), uses Docker for containerization
- **Deployment:** Can be self-hosted or used via provided hosting
- **Current Status:** Multiple branches exist—beta version on main branch, stable v4.x recommended for production use
- **Community:** Actively maintained with contribution guidelines

---

## Design Principle

Use technology in a way that strengthens—not harms—human relationships. Unlike major social platforms designed for engagement at any cost, Monica serves only you and your chosen connections.
